anitrip — Support

Anime pilgrimage planning & camera-reference app by GameSushi KK

anitrip helps you plan anime pilgrimage trips, view spot locations on a map, build routes between spots, and keep photo records of your visits. This page covers common questions and how to reach us.

Frequently Asked Questions

"Work data failed to load" / 作品数据加载失败

The app fetches spot reference data from ww.anitabi.cn. Make sure your device has a working internet connection and try again. The data is read-only reference content; your own plans are stored separately on your device.

Map or location is not working

anitrip needs Location permission to show your position and plan routes. Enable it in iOS Settings → anitrip → Location → While Using the App. A network connection is also required to load map tiles.

Where is my data stored?

All your plans, routes, and photos are stored only on your device. anitrip has no user accounts and does not upload your data to any server. Deleting the app removes your data.

How do I back up my plans?

Use the in-app share/export feature to save or move your data. Because data stays on-device, we recommend exporting before switching devices.
