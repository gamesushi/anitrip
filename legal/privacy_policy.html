anitrip — Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-08-12  ·  Last updated: 2026-08-12

anitrip is an anime pilgrimage planning and camera-reference app published by GameSushi KK. This policy explains what data the app handles, how it is used, and the choices you have. We wrote it in plain language because your privacy should be easy to understand.

1. Data We Collect

anitrip is designed to work primarily on your device. The only personal data the app touches is:

  • Precise Location. When you use the map or route features, anitrip requests access to your device's precise location to show your position, plan routes between pilgrimage spots, and display nearby locations. This happens only while you are using those features and only with your permission.
  • Photos & Camera. You may choose to attach photos (from your photo library or camera) to your own pilgrimage records. Images are added only when you explicitly select them.
  • Reference Content (not personal data). The app downloads read-only anime location and spot reference data from the anitabi services (ww.anitabi.cn, api.anitabi.cn) to display spot information. This is public reference content, not your personal data.

2. How We Use Your Data

Your location and photos are used only on your device to provide map display, route planning, and your personal pilgrimage records. We do not build profiles about you.

3. Data Storage & Security

All of your plans, photos, and location history are stored locally on your device in an on-device database (SQLite via drift). anitrip does not operate user accounts and does not transmit your personal data to our servers.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties. anitrip does not include third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

5. Children

anitrip is rated 4+ and is not directed specifically at children. The app does not knowingly collect personal data from children beyond the on-device data described above.

6. Your Choices

  • You can revoke Location or Photo permissions at any time in iOS Settings → anitrip.
  • Deleting the app removes all locally stored data permanently.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
