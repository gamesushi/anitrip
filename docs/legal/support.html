anitrip — Support

Anime pilgrimage planning & camera-reference app by BilyHurington

anitrip helps you plan anime pilgrimage trips, view spot locations on a map, build routes between spots, and keep photo records of your visits. This page covers common questions and how to reach us.

Frequently Asked Questions

"Work data failed to load" / 作品数据加载失败

The app fetches spot reference data from the public anitabi service. Make sure your device has a working internet connection and try again. If the network is slow or unavailable, the Explore tab falls back to a bundled offline snapshot so existing plans still work. The data is read-only reference content; your own plans are stored separately on your device.

Map or location is not working

anitrip needs Location permission to show your position and measure distances to nearby spots. Enable it in iOS Settings → anitrip → Location → While Using the App. A network connection is also required to load map tiles. You can switch the map source in Settings.

Where is my data stored?

All your plans, routes, and photos are stored only on your device. anitrip has no user accounts and does not upload your data to any server. Deleting the app removes your data.

How do I back up my plans?

Use the in-app share/export feature to save or move your data (plan package, CSV, or comparison card). Because data stays on-device, we recommend exporting before switching devices or uninstalling.

The reference image / spot location looks wrong

Open the spot, then use "Report location error" to submit a correction with your current coordinate and a note. Submissions are stored locally on your device and help the maintainer improve accuracy over time.

常见问题

「作品数据加载失败」

应用会从 anitabi 公开服务拉取巡礼点参考数据。请确认设备联网正常后重试。若网络较慢或不可用，探索页会回退到内置的离线快照，已有机计划仍可正常使用。该数据仅为只读参考内容，你自己的计划单独保存在本机。

地图或定位无法使用

anitrip 需要定位权限来显示你的位置并计算与附近巡礼点的距离。请在 iOS「设置 → anitrip → 位置 → 使用 App 期间」中开启。加载地图瓦片也需要联网。你可以在设置中切换地图源。

我的数据保存在哪里？

所有计划、路线与照片仅保存在你的设备本地。anitrip 没有用户账户，也不会把你的数据上传到任何服务器。删除应用即清除你的数据。

如何备份我的计划？

使用应用内的分享 / 导出功能保存或迁移数据（计划包、CSV 或对比图）。由于数据保存在本机，建议在更换设备或卸载前先导出。

参考图 / 巡礼点位置看起来不对

打开该巡礼点，使用「报告位置错误」提交修正（附当前坐标与说明）。提交内容仅保存在你本机，有助于维护者逐步提升数据准确性。
