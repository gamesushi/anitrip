anitrip — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-24

AniTrip ("the App") is developed and maintained by an independent developer, BilyHurington. This policy explains how the App handles your information.

Short version

The App has no accounts, no servers, no ads, and no third-party analytics or crash reporting. Your pilgrimage plans, visit records and photos stay on your own device.

1. What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login and no cloud sync, so no data that could identify you is ever sent to us or to anyone else.

2. Data stored on your device

The following lives in a local database (SQLite) created by the App. Deleting the App deletes it as well:

  • Pilgrimage plans, works and spots (including coordinates you add or edit)
  • Visit records, comparison photos you take and notes you write
  • App preferences (language, theme, map tile source, etc.)
  • Location reports you submit through "Report location error"
  • GPS track recordings (coordinate samples, duration and distance collected after you start recording manually)

This data leaves your device only when you explicitly make it happen:

  1. You use an export feature (plan package, CSV, comparison card);
  2. You share or save something through the system share sheet.

3. Device permissions

PermissionPurposeRequired?
Location (when in use)Show your position on the map, compute distances to nearby spots, read your current coordinate for location reports, and record your pilgrimage route (foreground only; pauses automatically when the App moves to the background)No
Photo library (read)Pick a screenshot as a comparison reference, attach a photo to a location reportNo
Photo library (add)Save comparison photos and exported pilgrimage cardsNo
CameraTake on-site comparison photosNo

Denying any permission only disables that feature. You can revoke permissions at any time in your system settings.

4. Third-party services contacted over the network

Some content comes from public data sources. Requests necessarily carry your IP address — that is how the internet works — and some providers may infer your approximate region from it. Nothing else identifying you is sent.

  • Pilgrimage data / reference images: the public anitabi dataset and its image service.
  • Cover art and metadata: the public bangumi.tv API (only when a cover is missing).
  • Nearest station names: the OpenStreetMap Overpass API.
  • Map tiles: OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap tile sources by default; you can switch to a custom XYZ or MapLibre style URL in Settings.

Each service is governed by its own privacy policy and terms. Once you switch map sources, tile requests go to the provider you configured.

Note: the App contains no analytics, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.

5. Children's privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

6. Retention and deletion

Everything is local. To erase your data you can either delete the relevant plan or record inside the App, or uninstall the App (which removes the local database and cached images). There is nothing to delete on our side — we have no servers.

7. Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces a networked feature, this policy will be updated and surfaced in the App. Continued use means you accept the updated policy.

8. Contact

Questions? Email bilyhurington@gmail.com.

anitrip — 隐私政策

最近更新：2026-10-24

AniTrip（以下简称「本应用」）由独立开发者 BilyHurington 开发与维护。本政策说明本应用如何处理你的信息。

一句话总结

本应用没有账户、没有服务器、没有广告、没有第三方统计或崩溃上报。你的巡礼计划、打卡记录与照片全部只保存在你自己的设备上。

一、我们收集的信息

我们不收集任何信息。本应用不存在注册、登录或云同步功能，因此没有任何可用于识别你身份的数据被发送至我们或任何第三方。

二、存储在你设备上的数据

以下内容保存在本应用在本机创建的本地数据库中（SQLite），删除应用即一并删除：

  • 巡礼计划、作品与巡礼点数据（含你手动新增或修改的坐标）
  • 打卡记录、你拍摄的对比照片与你写入的文字备注
  • 应用偏好设置（语言、主题、地图源等）
  • 坐标偏差反馈记录（你在「报告位置错误」中提交的内容）
  • GPS 轨迹记录（你手动开始录制后采集的坐标采样点、时长与距离）

这些数据仅在以下情形离开你的设备：

  1. 你主动使用导出功能（生成计划包文件、CSV 或分享对比图）；
  2. 你主动使用系统分享 / 保存到相册。

三、设备权限

权限用途是否必需
位置（前台）显示你在地图上的位置、计算与附近巡礼点的距离、「报告位置错误」时读取当前坐标、记录巡礼轨迹（仅前台，切换到其他应用时自动暂停）否，可拒绝
相册（读取）挑选截图作为对比参考图、为坐标反馈选择照片否，可拒绝
相册（写入）保存拍摄的对比照片与导出的巡礼卡片否，可拒绝
相机拍摄圣地实景对比照片否，可拒绝

拒绝任一权限只会导致对应功能不可用，其余功能不受影响。任何时候你都可以在系统设置中撤销权限。

四、联网时会访问哪些第三方服务

本应用的部分内容来自公开数据源，请求会携带你的 IP 地址（这是互联网通信的固有属性），部分服务可能据此判断大致地区。除此之外我们不会向它们提供任何标识你的信息。

  • 圣地巡礼数据 / 参考图：依据 anitabi 公开数据集及其图片服务。
  • 作品封面与元数据：bangumi.tv 公开 API（仅在封面缺失时用于解析封面图）。
  • 最近车站名称：OpenStreetMap Overpass API（用于推断巡礼点最近车站）。
  • 地图瓦片：默认使用 OpenFreeMap / OpenStreetMap 系列瓦片源；你也可以在设置中切换为自定义 XYZ 或 MapLibre 样式源。

上述服务各自适用其自身的隐私政策与服务条款。切换地图源后，瓦片请求将发往你所配置的服务商。

提示：本应用本身不含任何统计分析、崩溃上报或广告 SDK。

五、儿童隐私

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会主动收集任何年龄段用户的个人信息。

六、数据保留与删除

所有数据在你的设备本地。你可以通过以下方式彻底清除：

  • 在应用内删除对应计划 / 打卡记录；
  • 卸载应用（会同时清除本地数据库与缓存的图片）。

卸载后我们侧不存在任何需要删除的数据，因为我们没有服务器。

七、政策更新

若未来新增需要联网的功能，我们会在新版中更新本政策并在应用内提示。继续使用即视为接受更新后的政策。

八、联系我们

对本政策有疑问请发送邮件至 bilyhurington@gmail.com。
